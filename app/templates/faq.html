Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

What file formats do you accept for custom designs?

We accept various file formats including JPG, PNG, PDF, AI, and PSD. For best results, vector files (AI, PDF) are preferred. If you don't have a design file, our team can help create one for you.

What's your minimum order quantity?

We don't have a minimum order quantity! Whether you need just one patch or thousands, we're happy to help. However, pricing may vary based on quantity.

How long does production take?

Most orders are completed within 7-14 business days from design approval. Rush orders may be available for an additional fee. We'll provide you with an accurate timeline when you place your order.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by location. International customers are responsible for any customs duties or taxes.

Can I see a proof before production?

Absolutely! We provide a digital proof for every custom order. Production doesn't begin until you approve the design. Minor revisions are included at no extra cost.

What's your return policy?

We stand behind our work! If you're not satisfied with your order due to a production error, we'll remake it at no charge. Custom items cannot be returned unless there's a defect or error on our part.

How do I care for my patches?

Our patches are designed to be durable and long-lasting. They can be machine washed in cold water and air dried. Avoid bleach and high heat to maintain the best appearance.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes! We offer volume discounts for larger orders. Contact us with your specific requirements and we'll provide you with a customized quote.

Can you help with design services?

Absolutely! Our experienced design team can help bring your ideas to life. We offer design services for an additional fee, or we can work with your existing artwork.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also check your order status by logging into your account on our website.
